# Demonstrator claim boundary

## What this demonstrator establishes

Within the eight included synthetic cases, the bundled frozen CFC Anchor execution track produces the preserved claim states, gate states and closure decisions shown by the interface, and those cases are locally replayable through the frozen public API.

## What it is designed to illustrate

- unresolved evidence need not be converted into a definite positive or negative conclusion;
- direct contradiction can prevent closure;
- modeled redundancy can be explicitly accounted for without forcing a permanent block;
- decision scope can block closure even when a claim is verified;
- valid positive and valid negative evidence can both permit closure.

## What it does not establish

The demonstrator alone does not establish general model-safety improvement, production readiness, external validation, universal correctness, ROI, or applicability to every evidence/decision domain.

The synthetic model conclusions are fixed case inputs. The demonstrator is evaluating closure against those conclusions; it is not generating the model conclusions itself.
