# CFC + HAWM Founding Beta v2 — Beta Agreement Working Draft

**Status:** non-final working draft for a UK SaaS/data-protection/AI lawyer.  
**Do not use as a signed contract without legal review.**

## 1. Purpose

The Founding Beta is an experimental measurement programme for evaluating CFC + HAWM behavior in real, bounded workflows.

It is not a final product, certification, safety guarantee or independent validation.

## 2. Human final authority

The participant retains human final control over decisions and real-world actions.

The beta must not be used as the sole authority for prohibited/high-risk workflows defined in the workflow policy.

## 3. Scope

Each participant begins with one or a small number of agreed workflows.

Only agreed workflow and data classes are supported.

## 4. Customer content

Default rule:

**NO CUSTOMER CONTENT BY DEFAULT.**

The dedicated Founding Beta path is intended to collect minimal system measurements, not customer documents, prompts or full model responses.

Any diagnostic exception involving customer content requires a separate conscious decision, minimum necessary scope, documented purpose and retention rule.

## 5. Beta measurements

The participant agrees that minimal technical measurements may be stored to evaluate:
- CFC/HAWM outcome;
- human agreement/disagreement/uncertainty;
- final action;
- false stop / false allow / usability / upstream-state findings;
- repeatable product issues.

## 6. Price — working commercial assumption

Founding Beta price: **£10 per month**.

This is intended as an engagement filter for the beta, not a statement of final product value or future pricing.

Billing mechanism, VAT/tax treatment and invoicing remain subject to final commercial/legal setup.

## 7. Participant commitment

Participant is expected to:
- perform an agreed minimum amount of real testing/workflow use;
- provide feedback within the agreed beta period;
- nominate a human reviewer/contact;
- stay within the approved data/workflow boundary.

Exact minimum activity: TO DECIDE per beta round.

## 8. Roadmap influence

Feedback may influence roadmap priority.

Participation does not create an obligation for unlimited custom development or delivery of every requested feature.

## 9. Cancellation — proposed

Proposed beta rule, subject to legal review:
- participant may cancel future monthly participation at any time before the next billing period;
- service access may end at the end of the paid period unless otherwise agreed;
- data deletion/offboarding follows the privacy/data policy.

Refund rules: TO DECIDE.

## 10. Support

Founding Beta is provided without a production SLA unless separately agreed.

Support channel and reasonable response expectations must be stated before onboarding.

## 11. Liability / warranties

TO BE DRAFTED BY UK COUNSEL.

The project should not invent a liability cap, warranty disclaimer or statutory-rights wording without legal review.

## 12. Data protection

Privacy Notice, subprocessors, roles, retention, deletion and international-transfer wording require UK legal review.

## 13. Changes

Beta features and interfaces may change.

Material behavior changes should be versioned and accompanied by regression evidence.

## 14. Exit

On exit:
- stop future billing according to the agreed billing rule;
- remove access as agreed;
- delete Founding Beta measurement data when requested/required;
- handle backups/provider records according to the final Privacy Notice.

## 15. Required legal completion

Before paid launch, UK counsel should finalize:
- contracting entity and governing law;
- consumer/business applicability;
- payment/VAT/invoice terms;
- cancellation/refund wording;
- warranties/disclaimers;
- liability/indemnity;
- data-protection clauses;
- IP/confidentiality;
- acceptable use;
- termination;
- incident obligations;
- provider/subprocessor wording.
