# CFC + HAWM Founding Beta v2 — Participant Invitation

## Short version

We are opening a very small Founding Beta for CFC + HAWM.

The beta is not a finished product and it is not an external validation claim. Its purpose is to test one narrow control problem in real workflows:

> Does the available evidence and current system state actually justify a definitive conclusion before action?

We are looking for a small number of companies willing to test one bounded workflow with human final review.

## What participants do

A participant agrees to:

- choose one low-risk, bounded workflow;
- keep a human as the final decision-maker;
- run a small number of real cases;
- record whether CFC + HAWM was correct, too conservative, too permissive or unclear;
- provide short structured feedback;
- stay within the approved data boundary.

## What the beta collects

The dedicated Founding Beta path records minimal technical measurement only:

- system version;
- workflow type;
- non-sensitive case ID;
- CFC result: ALLOW / STOP / UNRESOLVED;
- reason code;
- HAWM state/presentation code;
- human assessment: AGREE / DISAGREE / UNSURE;
- final action;
- problem classification;
- optional short anonymized comment.

Default rule:

**NO CUSTOMER CONTENT BY DEFAULT.**

Do not submit customer documents, full prompts, full model replies or raw evidence text into the beta measurement path.

## What the beta does not claim

Founding Beta is not:

- a certification;
- independent validation;
- a production safety guarantee;
- a replacement for human review;
- a sole authority for high-risk decisions.

## Suitable workflows

- research validation;
- document/policy consistency review;
- AI-output QA;
- technical/vendor evidence checks;
- historical-case replay using sanitized inputs.

## Commercial model

Working Founding Beta assumption: **£10/month**.

This is intended as a small engagement filter, not final product pricing.

Paid activation remains subject to final legal/privacy/commercial setup.

## Participation

Initial target:

- 3–5 genuinely engaged companies to start;
- up to 10 in the first group;
- rollout in waves if support/feedback becomes heavy.

Each company starts with one agreed workflow and one named human reviewer.
