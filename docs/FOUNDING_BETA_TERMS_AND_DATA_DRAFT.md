# CFC + HAWM Founding Beta — Terms & Data Working Draft

**Status:** product draft only. This is not legal advice and is not a substitute for legal/privacy review.

## Beta status

The service is an experimental Founding Beta.

Features, interfaces, limits and behaviour may change during the beta.

Participation does not imply certification, external validation or a production-safety guarantee.

## Human responsibility

The participating company remains responsible for human review and for decisions made in its workflow.

The beta should not be used as the sole decision mechanism for high-risk actions.

## Scope

Each participant should begin with one or a small number of agreed workflows.

Use outside the agreed beta scope may not be supported.

## Feedback

Participants may provide bug reports, usability observations, missing-feature requests and workflow findings.

Feedback may be used to improve the product and prioritize the roadmap.

Participation does not create an obligation to deliver unlimited custom development.

## Data categories to decide before launch

For each beta workflow, document:
- what input data is submitted,
- whether personal data is present,
- whether sensitive/special-category data is present,
- what model/provider receives data,
- what CFC + HAWM stores,
- what logs are retained,
- retention duration,
- deletion process,
- who can access stored records,
- where infrastructure is hosted.

## Initial data rule

Until a reviewed data policy is complete, use synthetic, public, anonymized, or otherwise appropriately authorized low-risk data.

## Retention

A concrete retention period must be chosen before commercial onboarding.

Retention should be no longer than needed for:
- replay,
- debugging,
- regression testing,
- agreed beta support.

A deletion mechanism and deletion confirmation process must be defined.

## Security and confidentiality

Access to beta workspaces and persisted records should be limited to authorized participants and project operators.

Secrets, passwords, API keys and unrelated confidential data must not be entered into feedback reports.

## Availability and support

Founding Beta is not an SLA-backed production service unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

Expected support channel and response expectations must be stated before onboarding.

## Pricing

The working Founding Beta concept uses a symbolic entry price rather than a final product valuation.

Any actual charge requires a payment flow, billing/tax handling and confirmed terms before collection.

## Exit

Participants should be able to leave the beta.

The offboarding process should define:
- account/workspace closure,
- export if offered,
- deletion/retention of stored data,
- treatment of previously submitted feedback.

## Required pre-launch decisions

This draft must be completed with concrete answers for:
- controller/service version offered,
- payment mechanism,
- jurisdiction and contracting entity,
- privacy notice,
- retention period,
- deletion process,
- support contact,
- incident contact,
- permitted/prohibited workflow classes.
