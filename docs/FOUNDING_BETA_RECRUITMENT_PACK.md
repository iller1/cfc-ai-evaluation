# CFC + HAWM Founding Beta v2 — Recruitment Pack

## One-sentence description

CFC + HAWM Founding Beta tests whether the evidence and current state actually justify closing an AI-assisted conclusion before a human acts on it.

## What makes the beta different

- human remains the final decision-maker;
- **NO CUSTOMER CONTENT BY DEFAULT**;
- structured evidence state rather than document collection;
- short-lived source measurements;
- automatic 14-day archive-then-delete retention;
- retained learning is aggregate and content-minimized;
- known limitations are disclosed rather than hidden.

## Privacy/security posture

> We use data minimisation by design. If we do not need customer content to evaluate the control behaviour, we do not collect it.

> We are transparent about how the system works, what data it uses, what it retains and where its current limits are. We do not expose security-sensitive implementation details or information that could compromise customers or the service.

Do not use absolute claims such as `maximum security`, `fully secure`, `zero risk` or `GDPR compliant` unless a qualified review supports the exact statement.

## What a participant needs

- one bounded, low-risk workflow;
- an existing AI-assisted conclusion or review step;
- a human who can agree/disagree with the CFC/HAWM result;
- the ability to use synthetic, public, authorized or sanitized inputs;
- willingness to run several real cases and give structured feedback.

## Good first-wave examples

- AI-output QA;
- research/evidence review;
- policy/document consistency checks;
- technical or vendor evidence checks;
- replay of historical sanitized cases.

Avoid first-wave workflows requiring sensitive personal data, autonomous action, regulated high-risk decisioning or deep production integration.

## Working first-wave expectation

- one agreed workflow;
- one named human reviewer;
- approximately 5 real bounded cases during the beta period;
- one short structured feedback summary;
- immediate reporting of false allow, false stop, unexpected persistence or access problems.

The exact minimum activity remains an owner/commercial decision until formally confirmed.

## What we show them

Be highly transparent about data flow, what is and is not collected, 14-day source-measurement retention, deletion, human-final-control, architecture boundaries, known limitations, observed test results and unresolved external facts.

Do not disclose secrets, credentials, security-sensitive details that would materially aid attack, another participant's data, private unrelated logs or information not needed to evaluate the product.

## Initial outreach

I'm looking for companies that already use LLMs in review, research, QA or decision-support workflows and can test one bounded low-risk use case with a human final reviewer. The dedicated beta uses structured state and follows NO CUSTOMER CONTENT BY DEFAULT. If you have a workflow where premature closure is a real concern, I'd be interested in testing a handful of cases together.

Do not send the full legal/technical pack in the first message unless requested.

## Qualification outcome

Every prospect should end in one of: ACCEPTED, ACCEPTED WITH CONDITIONS, NOT YET ACCEPTED or DECLINED.

Do not pressure a workflow into the beta merely to increase participant count.

## Wave-1 success

Success means real cases are run, human comparisons are recorded, findings are reproducible, repeated patterns appear across independent workflows, fixes are versioned/regression-tested and participants find enough value to continue.
